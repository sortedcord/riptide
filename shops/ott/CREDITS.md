All assets were generated for Riptide. There are no photos, no third-party images and no hotlinks.

- **Poster and backdrop art**: inline SVG generated at render time by `components/ui.tsx` `Poster`, from each title's seeded palette and genre motif (`lib/catalogue.ts`). Generated for Riptide.
- **Reelbox logo and favicon** (`components/ui.tsx` `Logo`, `app/icon.svg`): generated for Riptide.
- **Icons**: inline SVG paths drawn for Riptide (`components/ui.tsx` `Icon`).
- **Catalogue**: 64 fictional titles, loglines, cast and episode names written or seeded for Riptide. Any resemblance to real titles or people is unintended.
